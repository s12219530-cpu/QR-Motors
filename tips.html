ESSENTIAL CHECKS
01 — INSPECTION GUIDE

Six checks before
you make an offer.

Use this sequence in order. Begin with ownership and documentation, move through mechanical condition, then finish with value and professional verification.

01DOCUMENTS
⌑

Verify the legal identity

Match the license, ownership papers, VIN, insurance details, and seller identity before discussing payment.

  • Ownership matches seller
  • VIN is consistent
  • No missing documents
02ENGINE
⚙

Inspect from a cold start

Listen for unusual noise and check oil, coolant, smoke, leaks, vibration, and warning lights.

Never inspect only after warm-up
03ROAD TEST
↗

Drive in real conditions

Test braking, steering, transmission, suspension, acceleration, visibility, and comfort on different roads.

04MARKET VALUE
₪

Price the condition, not the badge

Compare year, mileage, trim, condition, and service history against similar cars before negotiating.

Open comparison tool →
05MILEAGE
◴

Check whether mileage makes sense

Compare odometer mileage with age, interior wear, service records, tires, and previous inspection reports.

06EXPERT CHECK
✓

Let a mechanic confirm it

A trusted independent mechanic can identify hidden faults and estimate the real repair cost before payment.

  • Computer diagnostics
  • Underbody inspection
  • Repair-cost estimate
02 — FINAL WALKAROUND

buying checklist.

Do not transfer money until every critical item is checked.
